Grant Allocator FAQ

Frequently Addressed Questions

How are fellowship candidates vetted?

Candidates undergo a three-stage filter: proof-of-work code sample review, an algorithmic coding assessment, and an architectural interview with a guild core contributor. Acceptance rates across recent cohorts average 6.4%.

Can our foundation provide direct technical mentors?

Yes. Cohort tracks welcome your DevRel engineers and core protocol contributors for guest lectures, office hours, and PR reviews. We organize scheduling and ensure asynchronous recordings for all time zones.

Can we fund hacker houses in specific regions only?

Absolutely. You may sponsor dedicated houses specifically in Nairobi, Lagos, Accra, or Kigali, or propose a nomadic multi-city sprint depending on where your protocol sees highest developer concentration.

What currencies and payment rails do you accept?

We accept standard corporate wire transfers (USD/EUR) as well as on-chain stablecoin grants (USDC/USDT on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, and Solana) via audited multi-signature treasuries.
