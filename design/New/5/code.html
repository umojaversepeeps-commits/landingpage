AfriPay Rollup

Open-source protocol enabling cellular base-band settlement. Built by Kampala & Lagos Guild Working Group (Guild Lead: Kwame A., Amina O.).

Structural Problem

Cross-border payments across East and West Africa face exorbitant remittance fees (6–10%) and frequent cellular telecom gateway downtime on 2G infrastructure.

Sovereign Architecture

A lightweight USSD client coordinating peer-to-peer state channels on Ethereum Layer-2, designed to execute cryptographic transactions over intermittent GSM networks.
