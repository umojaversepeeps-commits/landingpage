# Umojaverse — Google Stitch Design Instructions

Design a refined, distinctive, multipage website for Umojaverse—an African developer community where people learn, collaborate, and build technology.

Study these references and combine their strongest qualities into an original design:

- https://www.southparkcommons.com/
- https://stationf.co/
- https://superteam.fun/
- https://www.kernel.community/

Draw from South Park Commons’ visual personality and storytelling, STATION F’s confident photography and presentation, Superteam’s clear participation pathways, and Kernel’s emphasis on community members. Create a coherent Umojaverse identity rather than stitching together copied sections.

## Brand and visual direction

Use the supplied Umojaverse logo. Preserve these brand colors:

- Purple `#693693` — primary brand color and main actions.
- Cyan `#1D9FD9` — selective accents and highlights.
- Off-white `#FCFCFC` — dominant background.
- Ink `#0D0E10` — primary text.

Aim for understated confidence, clean minimalism, class, and sleek execution. Use generous whitespace, expressive typography, precise alignment, and carefully balanced compositions. Let purple anchor the identity and cyan add energy; avoid giving both equal prominence everywhere.

Develop a subtle graphic language from the logo’s diagonal geometry. Use it selectively in image crops, section details, and transitions. Make the layout recognizable through composition and typography rather than decoration.

Use an elegant, highly readable sans-serif type system with strong size contrast. Combine occasional oversized headlines with concise body text. Prefer editorial layouts, open columns, fine separators, and varied image proportions over repeated card grids.

## Pages and content

Create complete, consistent designs for:

- **Home** — concise introduction, selected programs, one community/project story, and a clear invitation to join.
- **Programs** — workshops, campus activities, and learning opportunities, with individual program detail pages.
- **Projects** — community work, with project detail pages explaining the problem, builders, and outcome.
- **About** — purpose, people, and community story.
- **Partners** — collaboration opportunities and a focused enquiry form.
- **Join** — explain who the community welcomes, what members gain, and how to participate.

Keep the homepage short and curated. Move detailed information to its relevant page.

Open with: “Africa’s builders. Learning and creating together.”
Pair this with an authentic community photograph and clear actions: “Join the community” and “Explore programs.”

## Writing and experience

Make the purpose of every page immediately understandable. Use plain language, short paragraphs, descriptive labels, and clear navigation. Give real people and their work prominence. Use supplied or verified content; mark missing material as placeholders instead of inventing statistics, partners, testimonials, or events.

Design polished desktop and mobile versions with accessible contrast, visible focus states, comfortable touch targets, and restrained motion. Avoid neon glow, glass panels, decorative dashboards, and heavy 3D effects.

## Implementation constraints

Target Next.js App Router, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, hosted on Vercel. Do not use Vite. Use real page routes, reusable components, optimized imagery, and lightweight interactions.

The finished design should feel distinctive, welcoming, and meticulously composed, while remaining fast and effortless to understand.
