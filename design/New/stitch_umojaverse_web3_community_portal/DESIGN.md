## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a refined, minimalist editorial aesthetic created for African tech builders, creative technologists, and sovereign digital infrastructure. Rejecting tired Web3 tropes—such as gratuitous dark-mode glows, cyber noise, and speculative hype language—it introduces an architectural, intellectual, and grounded gallery tone.

The aesthetic philosophy draws inspiration from Swiss International typography and contemporary African architectural geometry:
- **Architectural Discipline:** Grounded in pure spatial balance, precise hairline grids, and purposeful diagonal accents directly derived from the emblem's diagonal bisection.
- **Warm Editorial Atmosphere:** Clean expanses of off-white and gallery paper create space for high-signal documentation, builder tools, and cultural artifacts without visual distraction.
- **Authentic Grounded Language:** Communicates in plain, human, and direct prose. Interface elements avoid decorative clutter and hyper-inflated statistics, centering human capability and institutional durability.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated for high-contrast legibility, meeting WCAG AAA compliance across primary reading contexts while deploying selective, high-resonance brand signals.

- **Primary Action (`#693693`):** Deep Regal Purple derived from the logo's grounding wedge. Applied strictly to primary actions, selected indicators, and sovereign identity markers.
- **Accent / Tech Signature (`#1D9FD9`):** Cyan Blue taken from the emblem's upper triangular wing. Serves as a technical signature for verified states, live connectivity badges, network confirmations, and interactive micro-highlights.
- **Surfaces & Tonal Layers:** Built on a layered light foundation: pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards and viewports, off-white (`#FCFCFC`) for base canvas backgrounds, and light gray-stone (`#F6F6F8`) for structural groupings.
- **Text & Contrast:** Obsidian Black (`#0D0E10`) ensures supreme reading contrast. Secondary text (`#4B5563`) handles supportive descriptions and subheadings, while muted neutral (`#9CA3AF`) is reserved for metadata and inactive cues.
- **Structural Lines:** Crisp, hairline borders (`#E5E7EB`) govern all division lines without generating visual noise.

## Typography

The type system blends technical confidence with human-centered legibility through a dual-sans pairing:

- **Headlines & Display (Space Grotesk):** Provides structured, geometric impact. Display and headline levels must always be set in sentence case with tight tracking to accentuate architectural weight and confident editorial tone.
- **Body & Continuous Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clear legibility across long-form reading, documentation, dashboards, and input fields. Its generous geometric curves balance the sharper mechanics of the display typeface.
- **Labels, Metadata & Indicators (Space Grotesk Uppercase):** Category markers, technical properties, statuses, and field titles are typeset in small Space Grotesk caps with wide tracking (+0.05em to +0.08em), acting as navigational coordinates across pages.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an asymmetric 12-column architectural grid on desktop, scaling down to a 6-column rhythm on tablet, and a 4-column flow on mobile.

- **Editorial Rhythm:** Layouts avoid monotonous uniform grids. Instead, broadsheet-style pairings (such as an 8-column primary work surface juxtaposed with a 4-column metadata rail) structure information hierarchically.
- **Hairline Framing:** Sections are partitioned by unbroken 1px rules in `#E5E7EB`. Borders span the full width of gutters and containers, serving as the structural skeleton of the canvas.
- **Whitespace Discipline:** Wide section margins (`3rem` on desktop) ensure expansive breathing room, establishing an unhurried, institutional clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed strictly through planar architecture, fine outlines, and surface shifts rather than diffuse drop shadows.

- **Zero-Shadow Construction:** Interfaces discard standard blurred elevation shadows. Floating elements, cards, and modal windows remain completely flat, using precise 1px `#E5E7EB` hairlines to delineate their physical boundaries.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Depth is achieved by placing `#FFFFFF` surface containers above the `#FCFCFC` canvas background, with `#F6F6F8` recess panels used for secondary ledgers and grouped inputs.
- **Architectural Offset Shadows (Optional Accent):** When a physical separation is required for floating popovers or primary dialogue boxes, an unblurred solid offset shadow (`2px 2px 0px #0D0E10` or `3px 3px 0px #693693`) may be used to reinforce tactile print precision.
- **Interactive State Cues:** Hover, active, and focus transitions do not elevate via vertical translation. Instead, state changes rely on sharp border color transitions, background color fills, and crisp line inversions.

## Shapes

This design system adopts a crisp `0` (Sharp) shape philosophy.

- **Orthogonal Geometry:** Buttons, cards, modals, tags, and inputs use strictly 0px border-radius (`rounded-none`). This reinforces structural precision and print-editorial character.
- **Diagonal Notches (Signature Accent):** Key call-to-action buttons, high-level featured modules, or identity badges incorporate a deliberate diagonal corner cut (45-degree chamfer, 8px to 12px) on the top-right or bottom-left corner using CSS clip-paths, directly quoting the logo's diagonal partition.
- **Hairline Enclosures:** Key containers leverage fine perimeter strokes, at times flanked by an exterior structural rule or bracket line to mirror the framed framing motif in the brand mark.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid purple (`#693693`) with sharp 90-degree corners and white (`#FFFFFF`) text set in Space Grotesk. Hover state transitions cleanly to `#0D0E10`. Optional signature variant features an 8px diagonal chamfer cut on the top-right corner.
- **Secondary Action:** Crisp off-white surface with a 1px solid `#E5E7EB` hairline frame and `#0D0E10` typography. Hover shifts background to `#F6F6F8` with a 1px `#0D0E10` border.
- **Technical Action / Cyan Signature:** Bordered with 1.5px `#1D9FD9` and filled with transparent surface, utilizing `#1D9FD9` uppercase text for protocol actions and chain verifications.

### Input Fields
- Razor-sharp 1px `#E5E7EB` outline over `#FFFFFF`. Zero corner radius.
- Padding: 12px 16px. Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans 15px.
- Focus State: Replaces outline with a precise 1.5px `#693693` border with zero blur or outer ring.
- Labels: Placed above the field in uppercase Space Grotesk (`label-sm`, tracking +0.08em) using `#4B5563`.

### Cards & Modular Panels
- White (`#FFFFFF`) background surface bordered on all sides by 1px `#E5E7EB`.
- No elevation shadow. Media and hero headers sit edge-to-edge with no internal border radius.
- Content divisions within the card are segmented using hairline borders rather than background pills.

### Chips & Protocol Badges
- Strict rectangular blocks with 0px corner radius.
- Standard Chip: `#F6F6F8` background with a 1px `#E5E7EB` border and Space Grotesk 11px uppercase text in `#4B5563`.
- Active/Live Badge: Integrates a solid 6px Cyan (`#1D9FD9`) square dot next to the label to signify real-time status without neon effects.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkbox: Sharp 16px by 16px square with 1px `#0D0E10` or `#E5E7EB` border. Checked state fills with `#693693` housing an uncurved geometric white checkmark.
- Radio Button: Pure square format enclosing a centered 6px inner solid purple square when activated.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- High-density layout separated by 1px `#E5E7EB` horizontal rules.
- Headers typeset in uppercase Space Grotesk with muted secondary tones (`#4B5563`).
- Row hover triggers an immediate background shift to `#F6F6F8` with zero vertical movement.
